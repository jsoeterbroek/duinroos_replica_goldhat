Fig. 16: Berlin Gold Hat. Apex and upper section of the shaft.

a. The apex (Zone 1) features an eight-pointed star set against a dotted background, positioned above a band containing 22 double-circle punch marks (Zone 2).

b. Zone 3 marks the beginning of the shaft, featuring 14 bosses, each enclosed within three circles. This is followed by Zones 4 through 9, which are separated from one another by notched ridges and beaded bands. The fifth zone displays a double frieze of horizontal crescent moons with central dots and eye motifs, whereas the other zones are covered with precisely stamped boss discs—featuring alternating patterns of 3 and 5 concentric rings—arranged in a continuous, repeating design.

From Zone 7 onwards, circles of dots appear in the interstices between the boss discs.
