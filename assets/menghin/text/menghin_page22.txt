Fig. 17: Berlin Gold Hat. Lower section of the shaft, cap, and brim:

a. The lower section of the shaft—which has a diameter of 10.8 cm at the base—is structured similarly to the upper section, with decorative zones situated between notched ridges framed by beaded bands. Above the pleated, cuff-like transition to the cap, it displays a frieze of large boss discs framed by only two rings; above this—in the 15th zone counting from the apex—lies the first band of large bosses with 6 rings. Between these large bosses, small "dot-bosses" (featuring two concentric circles) are stamped into the interstices—matching the style of the marks found in Zone 2 bordering the apex's eight-pointed star.

b. On the cap, the ornamental zones are separated from one another by bands of dots. The uppermost frieze (Zone 17) consists of small bossed discs with two rings. The next two zones are decorated—in a manner analogous to the penultimate symbolic frieze on the shaft—with identical bossed discs featuring six rings.

c. The brim is set off from the crown by a fold containing an internal bronze hoop and a narrow, circumferential band of pleating. Separated by a narrow band of ring-punched marks—similar to the band separating the crown from the pleating at the base of the shaft—the brim features two zones of bossed discs encircled by three and five rings, respectively; these appear concave when viewed from above. The diagonally ribbed rim bead of the brim is formed by a square-sectioned, twisted bronze wire, around which the thin gold sheet is folded upwards.
