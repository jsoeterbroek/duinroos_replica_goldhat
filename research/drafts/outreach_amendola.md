Subject: Golden Hat of Schifferstadt — the deposit-location paper, and the Berlin hat

Dear Prof. Amendola,

I am building a 1:1 replica of the Berlin Gold Hat that will display the competing
calendar readings (Menghin's lunisolar, Schmidt-Kaler's Venus) as labelled
scenarios, with the uncertainties shown. Your 2021 *Journal of Skyscape
Archaeology* paper on the Schifferstadt deposit location is the most interesting
recent contribution I have found, because it argues from the *place* rather than
from the ornament counts.

Two questions, if you have a moment:

1. Have you extended the horizon analysis to the other hats (Ezelsdorf-Buch,
   Avanton), where the find context is weaker or lost? Is the Schifferstadt result
   robust to how many candidate sightlines are admitted?
2. Do you read your result as independent of, or complementary to, the band-count
   readings? Andreas Fuls (2008) found only Schifferstadt's *ornament* statistically
   selective among the four hats, which seems to point the same way.

With thanks for the paper,
[your name]
