# FINDING_2026-10-10_ezelsdorf-total-correction

**Status:** verified (correction of a false corroboration)
**Source:** Wolfram Kaiser's plate (2005), redrawn from Schauer 1986 and Menghin

**Subject:** the symbol total of the Golden Cone of Ezelsdorf-Buch

## The error

The earlier AI digest (`gold_hat.md`, now
`theories/secondary_digest_2026-10-08.md`) stated Ezelsdorf-Buch has
**"≈ 1,739 symbols in 23 bands"** — the *same* total as Berlin — and used it as
evidence that the other hats encode the same system.

## The fact

Ezelsdorf-Buch has ≈ **499** stamped symbols over ≈ **19** ornament zones. The
1739 is Berlin's ring-unit total, copied onto the other hat by a summarising
model. There is therefore **no** independent corroboration of the Berlin numbers
from Ezelsdorf.
