## Verbatim

Schlosser 2003, p. 47:

> "Damit ist die Länge des Mondjahres variabel. Es gibt nicht 'das' Mondjahr
> schlechthin, und die Zahl 354 macht nur im Mittel Sinn — nicht im Einzelfall!"

Schlosser 2003, p. 51 (summary):

> "The lunar year (on average 354.37 days) varies in isolated cases by more than a
> day. A prehistoric find with 354 markings can therefore have hardly been used as
> a current calendar. … The author pleads for the consultancy of an astronomer."

Peter-Röcher 2023, p. 135–136:

> "Auf die anderen drei bekannten Goldblechkegel … ließen sich die Rechenoperationen
> leider **nicht übertragen**; der Schifferstädter zeigte nur wenige kalendarisch
> annähernd verwertbare [Ergebnisse]."

## What it establishes

1. **The lunar year is not a fixed 354 days** — it varies by **more than 26 hours**
   across 1967–2003 (Schlosser's Table). 354 is a mean, not a per-year count.
2. **The Metonic rule is not exact** — it holds only ~2/3 of the time in his sample.
3. **The method is the target**: selecting, from many possible sums, exactly the ones
   that fit a pre-formed calendar concept. Schlosser quotes Menghin's own admission
   (2000, 71) that plausible products need "flexible Handhabung" of the switch-zones.
4. **The cross-hat test fails in print**, not merely "blocked here": Peter-Röcher
   records that the calculation could not be transferred to the other three hats.
