## What the held abstract establishes

The authors' 2013 AKAG abstract states their argument runs

> „von der Himmelsscheibe von Nebra über den Sonnenwagen von Trundholm **bis zu dem
> Berliner Goldhut**"

and that it works through **lunar eclipses → the Saros cycle → the derived Metonic
intercalation cycle**, with eclipse-type selection and combination possibly
indicating the time *and* approximate place of a future solar eclipse (their Thales
application).

So, on the authors' own summary:

- the 32/33 / eclipse-Metonic model is an **independent explanation of the same class
  of numbers** as Menghin's and Fuls's readings, and
- it **explicitly includes the Berlin Gold Hat** — it is not a Nebra-only claim.

## What it does *not* establish

The abstract has no per-zone arithmetic. Whether the 32/33 combination (or the
Saros/Meton structure) maps onto any **specific Berlin zone value** — the thing T3
asked — **cannot be answered from what is held.** The chapter (pp. 131–154, incl.
"Nebra und der Kalender" p. 134 and "Trundholm und die Finsternisse" p. 143) and the
2014 Tübingen paper *Die Zahlenkombination 32/33 als Indikator für einen
plejadengeschalteten Lunisolarkalender* remain to be obtained.
