Significant Deposit Location*, *Journal of Skyscape Archaeology* 7 (2021).

## Key content (from the published abstract)

- The four conical gold hats were probably used by a priestly caste performing
  ceremonies linked to astronomical/calendrical knowledge.
- From the Nebra disc's burial place the Sun sets on the Brocken at the summer
  solstice; Amendola asks whether the **Schifferstadt** spot has the same
  function, with the natural horizon of the Odenwald / Palatinate Forest replacing
  the artificial horizon of a circular enclosure.
- Conclusion of the abstract: the location "was a natural astronomical/calendrical
  viewing place" of the same functional type.

## Known traps in this source

- It concerns the **Schifferstadt** hat (the one with the clearest ornament
  statistics, per Fuls), and it argues about the *find location*, **not** about the
  Berlin hat's band counts. Do not present it as corroboration of Menghin's Berlin
  reading.
- It is landscape-based; the astronomically meaningful horizon is a property of
  the place, not proof that the ornament was used as a calendar.
