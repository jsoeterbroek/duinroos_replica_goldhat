# Source: Hansen & Rink 2008 — calendars and eclipses / the 32-33 lunisolar calendar

**What it is:** Rahlf Hansen & Christine Rink, *Kalender und Finsternisse — einige
Überlegungen zur bronzezeitlichen Astronomie*, in: G. Wolfschmidt (ed.),
*Prähistorische Astronomie und Ethnoastronomie* (Nuncius Hamburgensis 8),
Norderstedt 2008, pp. 401–431. ISBN 978-3-8370-3131-7. Also their related paper
*Die Zahlenkombination 32/33 als Indikator für einen plejadengeschalteten
Lunisolarkalender* (in: G. Wolfschmidt ed., *Der Himmel über Tübingen*, 2014).

## Known traps in this source

- The same volume also carries Fuls 2008; keep the two chapters distinct.
- The 32/33 combination (Pleiades intercalation) is invoked for the Nebra disc and
  for a wider lunisolar practice; it is not a direct reading of the Berlin hat,
  but it is a candidate independent explanation for the same class of numbers.
