# Source: Lorenz 2010 / 2011 / 2nd ed. — the modern Metonic reading

**What it is:** Thomas Lorenz, *Weltwunder Himmelsscheibe* (Jungjohann Verlag,
ISBN 978-3-932347-99-3, 2010) and its journal articles:
- *Kalender der Bronzezeit*, *Archäologie in Deutschland* Heft 5/2011.
- *Raum, Zeit und Zahl — Kalender und Mathematik der Bronzezeit*, in the
  exhibition companion *GlaubenssacheN — Kult und Kunst der Bronzezeit*,
  Bad Buchau 2011.
- *Nachtrag für die 2. Auflage — Der Berliner Goldhut* (the 2nd-edition afterword
  that reads the Berlin hat directly).

A modern popular-science Metonic reading, independent of Menghin's own
publication and often cited by museum-adjacent material.

## Key content (verified on the page image)

- The Berlin hat has **19 line-bands**; the large disc symbols form **12 friezes**
  (with small discs) + **7 friezes** (without) = 19 — read as 12 short and 7 long
  lunar years (12 × 12 + 7 × 13 = 235 lunations = the 19-year Metonic cycle).
- **19 special symbols** (sun/moon), a **5-frieze** yearly-intercalation group, and
  the 8-rayed star at the tip; "19 Scheiben × 5 Kreise = 235 (nach Menghin)".
- He notes Menghin's 2000 reading "war bisher nicht allgemein anerkannt, da sie
  verschiedenen Symbolen teilweise willkürlich Funktion oder Ornament zuordnete",
  then proposes that the split is already in the object's structure.

## Known traps in this source

- It is **popular science**, not a peer-reviewed measurement. Its "19 line-bands"
  and "12 + 7 friezes" are *new* counts that must be checked against the object;
  they may or may not coincide with Menghin's zones.
- It reuses Menghin's 19 × 5 = 235; it is therefore not independent evidence for
  the Metonic number.
- Its wider astronomy (Balkakra, Haschendorf, Stonehenge, Nebra) is the same
  author's contested hypothesis.
