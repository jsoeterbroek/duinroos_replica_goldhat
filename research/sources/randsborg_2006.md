# Source: Randsborg 2006 — Calendars of the Bronze Age

**What it is:** Klavs Randsborg, *Calendars of the Bronze Age*, *Acta Archaeologica*
77 (2006), 62–90. DOI: 10.1111/j.1600-0390.2006.00047.x. A synthetic discussion of
Bronze Age time-reckoning and the imagery (incl. the gold hats and the Kivik
grave), placing the calendars in a wider cosmological reading.

**Licence:** paywalled (Wiley).

## Known traps in this source

- The Crossref author field is unreliable here; verify the byline on the article
  page before citing "Randsborg".
- It is **context**, not a measurement of the Berlin hat.
- The quote used on Wikipedia ("The brimmed hat, likely of the Sun god, is also
  depicted on the rock-carving panels of the Kivik grave…") is an iconographic
  argument; it supports a sun-cult reading, not the arithmetic.
