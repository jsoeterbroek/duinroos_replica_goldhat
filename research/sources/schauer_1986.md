# Source: Schauer 1986 (and Menghin & Schauer 1983) — the print-only canon

**What it is:** the fundamental monographs that hold the raw per-zone counts,
ring counts, tool types and zone order for the non-Berlin hats:
- Peter Schauer, *Die Goldblechkegel der Bronzezeit — Ein Beitrag zur
  Kulturverbindung zwischen Orient und Mitteleuropa* (Römisch-germanisches
  Zentralmuseum, Monographien 8), Habelt, Bonn 1986, ISBN 3-7749-2238-1.
- Wilfried Menghin & Peter Schauer, *Der Goldblechkegel von Ezelsdorf —
  Kultgeräte der späten Bronzezeit*, Theiß, Stuttgart 1983, ISBN 3-8062-0390-3.
- Gerhard Bott (ed.), *Der Goldblechkegel von Ezelsdorf* (exhibition catalogue),
  Theiß, Stuttgart 1983, ISBN 3-8062-0390-3.

**Licence:** in copyright, out of print.

**Already on this machine?** No.

## Why it matters

The **cross-hat test** — fix the counting rule on Berlin, then apply it blind to
another hat — is blocked until we have the *ring counts per stamp* and the *zone
order* for Ezelsdorf-Buch, Schifferstadt and Avanton. Fuls 2004/2008 reconstruct
these from these monographs; without them we cannot re-run the test independently.
(The Ezelsdorf plate reproduced in `theories/menghin_lunisolar.md` §6 gives symbol
counts only, ≈ 499 in ≈ 19 zones, not rings.)

## Known traps in this source

- Menghin's and Fecht's typings of the Ezelsdorf ornaments **differ** (noted by
  Fuls 2004, p. 53). Record which typology a count comes from.
- The Ezelsdorf hat was cut up by the finder and lacks the brim; not every zone is
  reconstructable.
- Do **not** attribute a joint "Spurk, Gebhard, Schauer" study to this material —
  no such joint paper exists (see `sources/phantom_references.md`).
