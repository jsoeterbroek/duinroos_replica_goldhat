# Source: Schlosser 2003 — Bronze Age lunar calendars (the critique)

**What it is:** Wolfhard Schlosser, *Einige Anmerkungen zur Realität
bronzezeitlicher Mondkalender*, *Jahresschrift für mitteldeutsche Vorgeschichte*
(JSMV) 86 (2003), online 2022. DOI: 10.11588/jsmv.2003.1.89612.
CC BY-NC-ND. A critical review of claims that Bronze Age objects encode lunar
calendars, by the Bochum astronomer best known for the Nebra disc.

**⚠️ Name correction.** The project's earlier digest called him "Wolfgang
Schlosser". The correct name is **Wolfhard Schlosser** (1940–2022). He wrote about
the Nebra Sky Disc, lunar calendars and archaeoastronomy in general — **not**
specifically about the gold hats.

**Licence:** CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 (per the OJS landing page).

## Known traps in this source

- The author-name drift is the trap (see `lessons.md`). Also note he is *not* a
  gold-hat specialist; treat the paper as critique of method, not a measurement of
  the Berlin hat.
- 2003 print, 2022 online: cite the print year unless you mean the online
  publication date.
