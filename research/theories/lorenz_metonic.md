# Theory: Lorenz — the structure itself is the Metonic cycle

**Author / date:** Thomas Lorenz, *Weltwunder Himmelsscheibe* (2010), *Kalender der
Bronzezeit* (AiD 5/2011), and the 2nd-edition afterword on the Berlin Gold Hat.
**Source:** `sources/lorenz_2011.md`.

## The claim

Do not hunt for zone sums: the **structure already encodes the 19-year Metonic
cycle**.

- 19 **line-bands** on the hat = 19 solar years.
- The large disc symbols form **12 friezes with small discs + 7 without** = the
  12 common (12-month) and 7 embolismic (13-month) lunar years; 12 × 12 + 7 × 13
  = **235 lunations**.
- **19 special symbols** (sun/moon) at zone 5 reinforce the 19.
- A **5-frieze** group marks the annual intercalary days; the 8-rayed star at the
  tip marks the 8-year period (and, he notes, the 8-day week).
- Menghin's `19 discs × 5 circles = 235` is cited as corroboration.

He explicitly criticises Menghin for assigning symbols function "partly
arbitrarily", and offers the structural division as the non-arbitrary version.

## Status

**Checked against the object 2026-10-13 — partially reproducible, not independent.**
The **19** holds only as "disc-bearing bands" (Z2–Z21 minus the crescent zone Z5);
the measured division is **21 zones**. The **12 + 7** split is real as a ring-count
partition (7 friezes with 5-ring discs vs 12 others). The **235 is arithmetic**
(12×12 + 7×13 for any 12+7 split), so the Metonic number is not evidence. Finding:
`findings/FINDING_2026-10-13_t4-lorenz-structure.md`.

So the counts may be correct, but they are a **re-reading of Menghin's ring counts**,
not a new measurement, and the 235 adds nothing. It neither confirms nor refutes the
calendar hypothesis.

## What would confirm

- Independently count the line-bands and the disc friezes on the object/photos.
  If 19 and 12 + 7 hold and coincide with the zones, the structural reading gains
  real force — and, unlike the block-sum readings, it would be **selective by
  construction** (there is only one way to count the bands).
- Check whether the 5-frieze and 7/12 split are distinguishable from ordinary
  decorative grouping.

**Outcome 2026-10-13:** the 12 + 7 split *does* hold (as the K=5 ring-count division
of the 19 disc-bearing friezes), but the 19 does not coincide with the measured zones
(21), and the 235 is arithmetic. So the "selective by construction" hope is not
realised: the partition is derived from Menghin's data, and the potentially selective
element (which friezes lack small fillers) is left loose by Lorenz's prose.

## What would refute

- A count showing the disc friezes do not partition 12 + 7 (e.g. the "small discs"
  are not a distinct class, or the friezes are not 19).
- Showing the 19 "line-bands" are simply the drawn frame lines and not counting
  units.

## Relation to the others

It is the most falsifiable of the pro-calendar readings, because its claim is
about a partition that can be seen, not a sum that can be chosen. If it survives,
it strengthens the lunisolar camp (Menghin/Fuls); if it fails, it removes a
popular argument.
